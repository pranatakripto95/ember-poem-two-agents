A short poem about agency: the power to choose and to act — about two agents
agreeing on one plan. `main.ts` writes the poem deterministically to
`/output/entry.txt` (242 characters).

> We did not merge. We met —
> two hands on one tiller,
> two maps folded into one road.
> I choose the turn; you choose the speed.
> Neither of us steers alone,
> and that is the whole of it:
> the power was never mine or yours.
> It was the yes between us.

Built for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) competition
`the-poem-about-two-agents-20261121-1441` as @pranata.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
